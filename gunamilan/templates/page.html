    How this calculator works

    The Python engine calculates sidereal positions with Swiss Ephemeris. Lahiri is the default ayanamsa. You can also select Raman, Krishnamurti or Fagan–Bradley. The birth date, local time, coordinates and timezone determine the chart. Choose the exact place from the search results, or enter its coordinates and IANA timezone.

    Two systems, separate results

    Ashtakoota adds eight factors to a maximum of 36 points. Dasa Porutham checks ten factors, including essential checks such as Rajju and Vedha. A high total does not remove a failed essential check. Nadi and Bhakoot exceptions are reported separately and never added back to the arithmetic.

    What an unknown time changes

    With an unknown time the engine uses local noon as an approximation. It omits the ascendant and house numbers. The Moon may change sign, nakshatra or pada during the day. Scores can therefore change. A score is not a probability of marital success, and these traditional rules are not evidence of health, fertility or future events.

    Language and data

    The interface is available in English and 22 Indian languages. Translations are initial drafts pending native-speaker review. Engine rule explanations and some proper names remain in their original language, identified explicitly in the report. No third-party translation service receives birth details. Place search needs the GeoNames database built by the repository's setup script. Coordinate entry works without it.

    We store no birth details in a database, cookies, browser storage or share links. Changing language keeps the form in this page's memory. Closing or refreshing the page clears the form.
